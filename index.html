Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products, delivery, and services.

Teak, aluminum, synthetic rattan, and wrought iron are our most popular choices. Teak offers natural weather resistance, aluminum is lightweight and rust-proof, rattan provides a resort-style aesthetic, and wrought iron adds classic elegance. Visit our Materials Guide for detailed comparisons.

Yes! We offer free delivery within city limits for orders above ₹15,000. For bulk and custom orders, delivery and installation are complimentary regardless of order value. Standard delivery takes 5–7 business days.

Absolutely! We offer full customization for most products — including dimensions, material choice, cushion fabric, and finish color. Visit our Bulk & Customization Orders page or contact us for a design consultation.

Maintenance depends on the material. Teak requires seasonal oiling, aluminum needs occasional soap-water cleaning, rattan should be kept dry and wiped clean, and wrought iron benefits from annual touch-up paint. Detailed care guides are included with every purchase.

Our warranty covers structural defects, frame damage, and material degradation under normal outdoor use. It does not cover cushion fabric wear, scratches from physical impact, or damage from extreme misuse. Full terms are available at checkout.
