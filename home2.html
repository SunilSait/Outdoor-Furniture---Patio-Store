Know Your Materials

Each material has unique properties — learn which one is perfect for your space.

Teak Wood

Teak is the gold standard for outdoor furniture. Its natural oils make it incredibly resistant to moisture, insects, and rot. Over time, teak develops a beautiful silver-grey patina that adds character.
