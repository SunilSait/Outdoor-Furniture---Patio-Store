Materials Guide

Understanding materials is key to choosing furniture that lasts. Explore our comprehensive guide.

Premium Material

Teak Wood

The gold standard of outdoor furniture. Teak's natural oils make it exceptionally resistant to moisture, insects, and UV damage. Over decades, it develops a distinguished silver-grey patina.

Durability: Exceptional

Lasts 50+ years with minimal care. Naturally rot and termite resistant.

Weather Resistance: Outstanding

Natural oils repel water. Handles sun, rain, and humidity without warping.

Maintenance: Seasonal Oiling

Apply teak oil 1–2 times a year to maintain golden tone, or leave untreated for a natural patina.

Best for: Dining sets, garden benches, lounge chairs, side tables

Lightweight & Modern

Aluminum

The modern choice for hassle-free outdoor living. Powder-coated aluminum is incredibly lightweight, completely rust-proof, and comes in virtually any color or finish.

Durability: Very High

Won't rust, corrode, or degrade. Powder coating adds scratch resistance.

Maintenance: Almost Zero

Wipe with soap and water occasionally. No oiling, sanding, or painting needed.

Best for: Bistro sets, sun loungers, stacking chairs, bar furniture

Resort Style

Rattan (PE Wicker)

Synthetic PE rattan captures the warm, organic aesthetic of natural wicker while offering superior weather resistance. UV-stabilized fibers prevent fading and cracking even in harsh tropical climates.

UV Stabilized

Won't crack, peel, or lose color under direct sunlight for years.

Easy to Clean

Simply hose down or wipe with a damp cloth. Resistant to mold and mildew.

Best for: Sofa sets, daybeds, egg chairs, dining sets, poolside furniture

Timeless Elegance

Wrought Iron

The strongest and most ornate option. Wrought iron's weight means it stays put in wind, and its malleability allows for intricate, artistic designs that age gracefully.

Strength: Unmatched

Heaviest and sturdiest of all outdoor materials. Wind-proof and impact-resistant.

Maintenance: Annual Touch-Up

Requires annual rust check and touch-up paint. Use furniture covers in heavy rain seasons.

Best for: Garden benches, bistro tables, arbors, decorative accents
